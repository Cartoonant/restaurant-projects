01

Aperol Spritz

Spritz

Ingredients

  • Ice cubesto fill
  • Soda water30 ml
  • Orange wedge1
  • Aperol60 ml
  • Prosecco90 ml

Instructions

  1. Fill a large wine glass with ice cubes.
  2. Add a splash of soda water.
  3. Pour in the Aperol.
  4. Top with Prosecco.
  5. Gently stir to combine.
  6. Garnish with an orange wedge.

Glass: Large wine glass / Spritz glass

Bold & chilled

05

Cosmopolitan

Shaken

Ingredients

  • Fresh lime juice15 ml
  • Cranberry juice30 ml
  • Orange wedge1
  • Vodka40 ml

Instructions

  1. Add lime juice, cranberry juice, and vodka into a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds until well chilled.
  3. Double strain into a chilled martini glass.
  4. Garnish with an orange wedge.

Glass: Martini glass

06

Gin Basil Smash

Shaken

Ingredients

  • Fresh basil leaves8–10
  • Fresh lemon juice20 ml
  • Simple syrup20 ml
  • Gin60 ml

Instructions

  1. Muddle the basil leaves gently in the shaker.
  2. Add lemon juice and simple syrup.
  3. Pour in the gin.
  4. Fill with ice and shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.
  5. Double strain into an old-fashioned glass filled with ice.
  6. Garnish with a fresh basil sprig.

Glass: Old-fashioned / Rocks glass

07

Margarita

Shaken

Ingredients

  • Fresh lime juice30 ml
  • Saltfor rim
  • Lime wheel1
  • Tequila (blanco)50 ml
  • Triple sec20 ml

Instructions

  1. Rim a margarita glass with salt: moisten the rim with lime, then dip in salt.
  2. Combine lime juice, tequila, and triple sec in a shaker with ice.
  3. Shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.
  4. Strain into the prepared glass over fresh ice.
  5. Garnish with a lime wheel.

Glass: Margarita glass / Coupette

12

Martini White

Stirred

Ingredients

  • Lemon twistgarnish
  • Gin (or Vodka)60 ml
  • Dry vermouth10 ml

Instructions

  1. Chill a martini glass in the freezer or with ice water.
  2. Add gin (or vodka) and dry vermouth to a mixing glass filled with ice.
  3. Stir smoothly for 20–30 seconds until thoroughly chilled.
  4. Strain into the chilled martini glass.
  5. Garnish with a lemon twist.

Glass: Martini glass

13

Mojito

Muddled

Ingredients

  • Fresh mint leaves8–10
  • White sugar2 tsp
  • Fresh lime juice20 ml
  • Crushed iceto fill
  • Soda watertop up
  • White rum45 ml

Instructions

  1. Place mint leaves and sugar in a highball glass.
  2. Muddle gently to release the mint oils (don't shred the leaves).
  3. Add lime juice and stir to dissolve the sugar.
  4. Fill the glass with crushed ice.
  5. Pour in the white rum.
  6. Top with soda water and stir gently.
  7. Garnish with a mint sprig and lime wheel.

Glass: Highball glass

16

Swimming Pool

Blended

Ingredients

  • Pineapple juice60 ml
  • Coconut syrup20 ml
  • Heavy cream20 ml
  • Crushed ice1 cup
  • Vodka30 ml
  • White rum30 ml
  • Blue curaçao20 ml

Instructions

  1. Add pineapple juice, coconut syrup, heavy cream, and crushed ice to a blender.
  2. Pour in the vodka and white rum.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Pour into a hurricane glass.
  5. Gently pour blue curaçao over the top — it will sink and create the "pool" effect.

Glass: Hurricane glass / Poco grande
